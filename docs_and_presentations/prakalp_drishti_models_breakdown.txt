TIER 1: CORE DECISION ENGINE (Must-Have Live Demos)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. MODEL NAME: KAAL-CHAKRA (Probabilistic Schedule Risk & Survival Analysis)
   - ROLE: 
     Replaces misleading single-date deadlines with probabilistic forecast curves.
     It applies Reference Class Forecasting (RCF) to group ongoing infrastructure
     works into empirical cohorts based on Sector, Cost Band, and Terrain. Using 
     the open-source `lifelines` survival analysis library, it generates P10/P50/P80/P95 
     fan charts indicating true completion probabilities.
   - CONCRETE REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE: 
     A new Metro Project in Mumbai announces an official completion deadline of 
     December 2027. KAAL-CHAKRA evaluates historical metro project timelines across 
     similar dense urban terrains. It calculates that the official deadline has a 
     nominal completion probability of <18% (sitting in the 4th percentile of 
     historical classes). It flags a P50 (Expected) completion of June 2028 and a 
     P80 (Safe Provisioning) completion of March 2029 for capital budgeting.
   - SUGGESTED ENHANCEMENTS:
     * Dynamic Feature Refreshing: Connect a time-varying feature loop where the 
       survival probability distribution curve shifts live as key prerequisites 
       (like securing 90% land possession) are achieved.
   - PROPOSED ADD-ON MODEL: 
     * The Choke-Point Detector: Incorporate a tree-based SHAP (Shapley Additive 
       exPlanations) model alongside the survival curve to explicitly print out 
       the top variables (e.g., IMD Monsoon Departure) driving that specific 
       project's tail risk.

2. MODEL NAME: PRATIBIMB / SATYA (Independent Earth-Observation Evidence Signal)
   - ROLE: 
     Serves as an objective, multi-sensor verification engine that challenges 
     self-reported web forms via orbital monitoring. It processes Sentinel-2 
     (10m Optical) and Sentinel-1 SAR (Radar) imagery to calculate surface 
     footprint changes (using NDBI/NDVI spectral differencing) and vertical radar 
     backscatter variations over time.
   - CONCRETE REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE: 
     A private contractor executing a 100km linear highway corridor submits a 
     monthly Common Upload Form (CUF) claiming 74.0% physical progress to trigger 
     their milestone payout. PRATIBIMB pulls cloud-penetrating radar and optical 
     baselines, calculating that the active earthworks and paving cover only 
     31.2% of the coordinates. It triggers a 42.8 percentage-point Divergence Alert, 
     issuing a high-priority physical field audit recommendation.
   - SUGGESTED ENHANCEMENTS:
     * EO-Eligibility Classifier Hardcoding: Ensure your UI highlights why certain 
       projects (like underground tunnels, deep mining, or software platforms) 
       are marked "EO-Ineligible" to prevent the jury from viewing a 0% surface activity 
       reading as a false positive.
   - PROPOSED ADD-ON MODEL: 
     * Nighttime Lights Index: Integrate VIIRS (Visible Infrared Imaging Radiometer Suite) 
       nighttime light data. For mega-ports, industrial zones, and multi-shift power 
       projects, a reduction in night illumination footprints serves as a proxy 
       signal for active operational slowdowns.

3. MODEL NAME: SETU-GRAPH (Cross-Project Dependency & Systemic Exposure Engine)
   - ROLE: 
     Maps macro-infrastructure interdependencies using spatial buffers and logic 
     linkages. Built on GeoPandas, it establishes structural nodes to trace spatial 
     corridor overlaps and inter-sector value chains, quantifying downstream economic 
     contagion when an anchor project stalls.
   - CONCRETE REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE: 
     A dedicated coal evacuation railway line in Odisha faces an 18-month delay due 
     to forest clearance disputes. SETU-GRAPH traces the logistics graph to reveal 
     that this specific rail link feeds three critical thermal power plants and two 
     heavy manufacturing zones. The model instantly outputs: "Systemic Exposure Alert: 
     ₹8,420 Crore downstream economic asset value locked due to transport bottleneck."
   - SUGGESTED ENHANCEMENTS:
     * Weighted Network Edges: Don't just show connecting lines between project nodes. 
       Use varied line thicknesses to represent active operational flow volumes 
       (such as millions of metric tonnes of cargo or megawatts of power grids).
   - PROPOSED ADD-ON MODEL: 
     * Critical Path Isolation Model: Use network centrality algorithms (e.g., Betweenness 
       Centrality) to rank all 1,847 projects. This isolated list reveals the single 
       "Linchpin Project" in India that, if delayed by a single month, triggers the worst 
       multi-ministry gridlock nationwide.

4. MODEL NAME: PRAGATI-SAARTHI (Deterministic Governance Briefing Engine)
   - ROLE: 
     Automates the generation of official, audit-ready civil service executive briefs 
     for apex PMO reviews. It operates strictly on deterministic data pipelines to 
     eliminate LLM hallucinations, embedding direct data lineage to ensure institutional trust.
   - CONCRETE REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE: 
     Ahead of the monthly PRAGATI monitoring session, the Cabinet Secretary requests 
     an operational brief on the Western Dedicated Freight Corridor. PRAGATI-SAARTHI 
     instantly generates a structured, formatted executive brief. Every core metric, 
     such as the projected cost "₹73,000 Cr", is embedded with a clickable token. Clicking 
     it displays the exact raw SQL database query, record IDs, and data snapshots.
   - SUGGESTED ENHANCEMENTS:
     * Actionable PDF QR Codes: Generate a secure, encrypted QR code on the final PDF brief. 
       When an official scans it, they are instantly routed to the live, secure interactive 
       dashboard view of that specific project.

TIER 2: INTEGRITY & PRESCRIBED OPTIMIZATION (Should-Have Modules)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5. MODEL NAME: SATYA-KAVACH (Statistical Threshold Bunching & Clause 10CC Auditor)
   - ROLE: 
     Acts as an institutional integrity monitor. It runs statistical distribution density 
     tests (McCrary Bunching Signal) to detect projects engineered to intentionally bypass 
     cabinet audit thresholds, while auditing private contractor price variations against 
     strict statutory guidelines.
   - CONCRETE REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE: 
     Indian administrative law states that any project budget revision exceeding 20% 
     triggers a mandatory Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) re-appraisal. 
     SATYA-KAVACH scans the entire historical database and flags an unnatural statistical 
     density spike (a 1.49x ratio) of budget adjustments clustering at exactly 19.8% and 
     19.9%. It automatically flags these projects for audit avoidance. Concurrently, it 
     runs the CPWD Clause 10CC formula to ensure contractor claims do not exceed legal price 
     variation limits.
   - SUGGESTED ENHANCEMENTS:
     * Vendor Behavior Profiling: Map these 19.9% threshold-bunching patterns against 
       the unique IDs of private construction contractors to identify recurring bad actors 
       across separate ministries.

6. MODEL NAME: VITTA-VYUHA (Prescriptive Capital Allocation Optimizer)
   - ROLE: 
     Solves the complex decision problem of distributing finite public capital across 
     competing stuck projects. It utilizes a Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) 
     solver (HiGHS) to maximize national completion velocity under realistic legal and 
     operational boundaries.
   - CONCRETE REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE: 
     MoSPI is allocated ₹10,000 Crore in supplementary infrastructure funds. Instead of 
     distributing it evenly or via political lobbying, VITTA-VYUHA models each project's 
     risk-adjusted completion probability and agency burn rate. It outputs an optimal allocation 
     distribution within 2 seconds, while guaranteeing mandatory statutory parameters like 
     North-East Region regional funding floors.
   - SUGGESTED ENHANCEMENTS:
     * Interative "What-If" Sliders: Incorporate live parameter sliders on the UI frontend. 
       Let the judges alter the available pool of capex from ₹10,000 Cr to ₹5,000 Cr and observe 
       the MILP engine re-allocate the optimal project funding path dynamically.

TIER 3: RESEARCH & SCENARIO PROTOTYPES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

7. MODEL NAME: HETU (Experimental Policy Scenario Simulator)
   - ROLE: 
     An advanced causal inference testing lab using Double Machine Learning (DML) 
     architectures. It allows policymakers to test "counterfactual" regulatory decisions 
     by isolating major historical legal shifts as natural experiments.
   - CONCRETE REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE: 
     A MoSPI administrator wants to simulate the impact of altering land acquisition compensation 
     multipliers on railway timelines. HETU models the structural break caused by the Land Act 
     of 2013 (RFCTLARR). It isolates confounding factors and projects that increasing payouts 
     reduces acquisition delays by 14 months but expands tail-risk project cost variance by 12%.

8. MODEL NAME: ARTHA-NETRA (PSU Financial Leverage Early-Warning Feature)
   - ROLE: 
     Bridges corporate capital markets data with public asset monitoring. It tracks daily 
     Debt-to-Equity ratios, stock drawdowns, and market capitalizations of listed public sector 
     companies to catch corporate stress before it causes a site slowdown.
   - CONCRETE REAL-WORLD EXAMPLE: 
     ARTHA-NETRA detects that a power-sector PSU has experienced severe equity drawdowns 
     and carries a highly stressed Debt-to-Equity ratio of 227. When this PSU bids on or manages 
     a mega hydroelectric project, the system raises its baseline execution risk tier. Highly 
     leveraged corporate entities show a statistically higher rate of project modifications.

ADD-ON 1: THE DPR-QUALITY SCORER (Natural Language Processing Module)
   - THE MISSION: 
     The MoSPI problem statement explicitly proves that infrastructure projects rushed 
     through approval during pre-election cycles experience a 79.1% delay rate due to 
     flawed, incomplete Detailed Project Reports (DPRs).
   - HOW IT WORKS: 
     Deploy a localized, open-weight Large Language Model (e.g., Llama-3-8B / Mistral-7B) 
     via an offline LangChain pipeline to parse incoming PDF project proposals. Have the LLM 
     scan for missing statutory prerequisites, lack of comprehensive soil engineering records, 
     and vague environment maps. The system outputs a 0-100 "DPR Quality Score." If the 
     document scores below 60, it triggers an early alert flagging high vulnerability to 
     future structural overruns.

ADD-ON 2: VARSHA-SPEED (Weather Working-Window Optimizer)
   - THE MISSION: 
     Infrastructure works are tightly constrained by Indian seasonal weather patterns, 
     compressing the true active construction calendar.
   - HOW IT WORKS: 
     Utilize the 630 state-years of historical IMD monsoon rainfall departure data mentioned 
     in your dataset. Build a time-series model that projects regional work season windows. 
     If a highway project in Assam is hit with an extended monsoon season that runs 25 days over 
     the Long Period Average, the system scales down the expected physical capability for that 
     quarter. This provides highly accurate timeline projections aligned with physical climate realities.
