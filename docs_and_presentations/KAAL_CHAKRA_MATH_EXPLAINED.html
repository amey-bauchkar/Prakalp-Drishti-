2 The Simple English Explanation (The Tower Analogy)
The 100-Floor Tower Analogy:
Imagine a contractor promised to build a 100-floor tower in 4.5 years.
Nearly 4 full years have passed (88% of the agreed time is gone).
You visit the construction site today and discover they have only built 1 floor (1% progress)!

• Can they finish the remaining 99 floors in the 6 months left? Obviously impossible.
• If they continue at their current snail's pace (1 floor every 4 years), finishing 100 floors would take over 400 years.
• KAAL-CHAKRA recognizes that early work is sluggish, but also respects the harsh ground reality. It combines national historical data for power plants with this snail pace to compute that this project will realistically need ~29 years to actually finish, landing in December 2051.
