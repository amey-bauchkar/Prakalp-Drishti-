## 3. The 5 Core Mathematical & Algorithmic Engines

---

### 3.1 KAAL-CHAKRA: Probabilistic Schedule Risk & Competing Risks Survival Engine

#### 1. Mathematical Formulation
Replaces point estimates with a **log-logistic Accelerated Failure Time (AFT)** survival model -- fitted by penalised maximum likelihood under right-censoring (160 observed completions, 1,988 censored), then conditioned on sector friction, expenditure pace and contractor frailty. Weibull is **not** fitted; only the log-logistic family is estimated. See [CLAIMS.md](CLAIMS.md):

$$\log T_i = \mu + \boldsymbol{\beta}^\top \mathbf{x}_i + \sigma\,\varepsilon_i, \qquad \varepsilon_i \sim \text{Logistic}(0, 1) \text{ or Gumbel}$$

#### 2. Fine-Gray Competing Risks Cumulative Incidence Function ($CIF$)
Distinguishes between projects advancing to completion ($k=1$) versus terminal absorbing failures (foreclosure, legal abandonment, descoping) ($k=2$):

$$F_1(t \mid \mathbf{x}) = \Pr(T \le t, K = 1 \mid \mathbf{x}) = 1 - \exp\left(-\Lambda_1(t)\,e^{\boldsymbol{\gamma}^\top \mathbf{x}}\right)$$

#### 3. Conformalized Quantile Regression (CQR) Bounds
Emits guaranteed non-crossing, monotone quantile forecasts:

$$P_{10} \le P_{50} \le P_{80} \le P_{95}$$

- **$P_{10}$ (Best-Case)**: Accelerated execution under optimal weather and immediate funding.
- **$P_{50}$ (Median Expected)**: Calibrated realistic completion date.
- **$P_{80}$ (Prudent Contingency)**: Recommended provisioning target for Ministry of Finance.
- **$P_{95}$ (Tail Catastrophe)**: Extreme disruption horizon under multi-year systemic gridlock.

#### 4. Baseline Reset Camouflage Detection
Computes true cumulative cost overrun and true delay against the **Original Sanctioned Parameters** rather than latest rebaselined targets:

$$\text{True Cost Overrun} = \text{Revised Cost} - \text{Original Sanctioned Cost}$$
$$\text{Reset Count} = \mathbb{1}\{\text{RevisedCost} > 1.01 \times \text{OriginalCost}\} \times \text{Rebaseline Multiplier}$$

### 3.2 SETU-GRAPH: Max-Plus Cascade Algebra & Shapley Systemic Criticality

#### 1. Network Topology & Tarjan SCC Condensation
Constructs a graph $G = (N, E)$ with **1,197 multi-modal dependency links** connecting 2,207 projects across:
- **Statutory Clearances** (Forest NOC $\to$ Civil Works)
- **Physical Supply Chains** (Coal Mine Extraction $\to$ Thermal Power Generation)
- **Shared Spatial Corridors** (Highway corridor sharing Right-of-Way with Gas Pipeline)
- **Evacuation Lines** (Power Generation $\to$ Transmission Grid $\to$ Industrial Parks)

To eliminate economic feedback cycles (e.g. Coal $\leftrightarrow$ Power), the engine applies **Tarjan's Strongly Connected Components (SCC)** algorithm to produce a condensed Directed Acyclic Graph ($G^* = (N^*, E^*)$).

#### 2. Max-Plus Schedule Algebra & Float Propagation
In the max-plus semiring $(\mathbb{R} \cup \{-\infty\}, \oplus = \max, \otimes = +)$:
- **Forward Pass (Earliest Dates)**:
  $$\mathrm{ES}_j = \max_{i \in \mathrm{Pred}(j)} (\mathrm{EF}_i + \ell_{ij}), \qquad \mathrm{EF}_j = \mathrm{ES}_j + D_j$$
- **Backward Pass (Latest Dates)**:
  $$\mathrm{LF}_j = \min_{k \in \mathrm{Succ}(j)} (\mathrm{LS}_k - \ell_{jk}), \qquad \mathrm{LS}_j = \mathrm{LF}_j - D_j$$
- **Float Distinction**:
  $$\text{Total Float (TF)}_j = \mathrm{LS}_j - \mathrm{ES}_j$$
  $$\text{Free Float (FF)}_j = \min_{k \in \mathrm{Succ}(j)} (\mathrm{ES}_k) - \mathrm{EF}_j - \ell_{jk}$$

> **Key Rule**: Delays within **Free Float** are absorbed harmlessly. Excess delays violate **Total Float**, propagating down the network and freezing downstream asset value.

#### 3. Permutation Monte Carlo Shapley Criticality ($\varphi_j$)
Quantifies each project's marginal contribution to national locked capital:

$$\varphi_j = \sum_{C \subseteq N \setminus \{j\}} \frac{|C|!\,(n - |C| - 1)!}{n!} \Big[ v(C \cup \{j\}) - v(C) \Big]$$

- Satisfies the **Efficiency Axiom**: $\sum_{j \in N} \varphi_j = \text{Total National Locked Capital} = \text{₹23.97 Lakh Crore}$.
- Couples directly into VITTA-VYUHA: $\gamma_{j,s} = \varphi_j^{(s)}$.

### 3.3 VITTA-VYUHA: Two-Stage Stochastic LP with Rockafellar-Uryasev CVaR90

#### 1. Optimization Formulation
Formulated as a Two-Stage Stochastic **Linear Program** solved via the **HiGHS** simplex engine in under **$10\text{ ms}$**. Naming note: every decision variable is continuous (`integrality` is all-zero), because capital tranches are genuinely divisible. It is an LP and is labelled as one -- a true MILP would add a binary fund/defer indicator with a minimum viable tranche, a deliberate future upgrade rather than what runs today. This distinction is load-bearing for the duals below: **an LP has valid dual variables; a MILP does not**, so the shadow prices we publish are meaningful precisely *because* the formulation is continuous:

$$\min_{x, y, z, \lambda, \eta, \zeta, w, u} \quad (1 - \kappa) \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}} p_s L_s \;+\; \kappa \left[ \eta + \frac{1}{1 - \alpha} \sum_{s \in \mathcal{S}} p_s \zeta_s \right]$$

Subject to:
- **(C1) Budget Ceiling**: $\sum_{i} x_{i,t} \le B_t$
- **(C2) Statutory 10% North-Eastern Region (NER) Floor**:
  $$\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}_{\text{NER}}} \sum_t x_{i,t} \ge 0.10 \sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}} \sum_t x_{i,t}$$
- **(C3) Agency Absorptive Capacity**: $\sum_{i \in \mathcal{I}_a} x_{i,t} \le \mu_a \bar{b}_a \quad (\mu_a \approx 1.25)$
- **(C4) SOS2 Piecewise S-Curves**: Minimum viable tranches ($\underline{x}_i y_i \le X_i \le \overline{x}_i y_i$) with adjacent weight activations.
- **(C5) Dependency Coupling**: Downstream acceleration requires feeder completion ($z_j \le z_i$).
- **(C-CVaR)**: $\zeta_s \ge L_s - \eta, \quad \zeta_s \ge 0 \quad \forall s$.

#### 2. Dual Shadow Price Extraction ($\pi$)
Extracts economic multipliers by fixing binary decisions at the optimal incumbent:
- $\pi_{\text{budget}} = +0.699$: Marginal yield per additional ₹1 Crore of national capex.
- $\pi_{\text{NER}}$: Exact opportunity cost of the statutory North-East ring-fence.
- $\pi_{\text{agency}, a}$: Capacity-building value of expanding agency $a$'s absorption ceiling.

### 3.4 PRAGATI-SAARTHI: 3-Layer Zero-Hallucination & Merkle Proof Lineage

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        3-LAYER ZERO-HALLUCINATION ARCHITECTURE                         │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. FACT LAYER      ──► Typed scalars/intervals + SHA-256 SQL/data/model hashes         │
│ 2. ASSERTION LAYER ──► Deterministic business rules + template slots bound to fact_id  │
│ 3. RENDER LAYER    ──► Bilingual formatting (en-IN / CSTT Hindi) without LLM numerals  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### 1. RFC 8785 JSON Canonicalization (JCS) & SHA-256 Merkle Lineage
- Every metric is normalized via RFC 8785 deterministic ordering.
- Leaves are bound: $h_f^{\text{bound}} = \text{SHA256}(h_Q \parallel h_D \parallel h_M \parallel \text{JCS}(\text{value}))$.
- Merkle root $R$ is embedded in signed PAdES-LTV PDFs with clickable verification URLs.
- **Positional Sibling Inclusion Proofs**: Serves $O(\log n)$ sibling paths enabling independent single-metric verification.
- **Live Client-Side Tamper Defense**: The dashboard immediately detects and rejects client-side DOM alterations if recomputed hashes diverge from $R$.

#### 2. Bilingual Parity
Full parallel rendering in English and official Commission for Scientific and Technical Terminology (**CSTT**) Hindi (e.g. *निर्धारित तिथि प्राप्ति संभाव्यता*, *संशोधित लागत*).

## 7. Cryptographic Audit & Validation Suite

PRAKALP-DRISHTI enforces 8 strict CI lint rules (R1–R8):
1. **R1 (Fact Binding)**: Every numeric slot must reference a valid `fact_id`. Literal numerals in prose fail the build.
2. **R2 (Uncertainty Mandatory)**: Probabilities, dates, and forecasts must render with confidence intervals ($P_{10}–P_{95}$).
3. **R3 (Round-Trip Test)**: Rendered text numerals must match source fact values with 0.000% error.
4. **R4 (Locale Formatting)**: Correct `en-IN` grouping (₹ 2,20,700 Cr) and Hindi transliterations.
5. **R5 (Grammar & CSTT Agreement)**: Strict grammatical agreement from noun-gender tables.
6. **R6 (Staleness Badging)**: Outdated facts render visual staleness warnings.
7. **R7 (Classification Redaction)**: Redaction occurs at Fact Layer, not Render Layer.
8. **R8 (Model Drift Alert)**: Triggered if PIT Anderson-Darling tests exceed 99th percentile threshold.
