1. KAAL-CHAKRA Temporal Risk Engine

Accelerated Failure Time (AFT) log-logistic survival regression combined with split-conformal quantile forecasting for project completion timelines.

EQ 1.1 Log-Logistic Survival & Cumulative Hazard Functions
$$S(t) = \frac{1}{1 + \left(\frac{t}{b}\right)^a} = \frac{1}{1 + e^z}, \qquad H(t) = -\ln S(t) = \ln(1 + e^z)$$

Models the probability that a project duration exceeds $t$ months from sanction. $z = \frac{\ln(t) - \mu}{\sigma}$ is the standardized log-time deviation, where $\sigma = 1/a$ is the scale parameter and $\mu = \ln(b)$ is the median duration predictor.

EQ 1.2 AFT Penalized Log-Likelihood with Right-Censoring
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{pen}}(\boldsymbol{\theta}) = -\sum_{i=1}^N \left[ e_i \ln f(t_i \mid \mathbf{x}_i) + (1 - e_i) \ln S(t_i \mid \mathbf{x}_i) \right] + \frac{\tau}{2} \sum_{j=1}^P \beta_j^2$$

Objective minimized via L-BFGS-B to train the AFT coefficients across $N = 2,207$ projects. Uncompleted projects ($e_i = 0$) contribute their survival probability $\ln S(t_i)$, while completed projects ($e_i = 1$) contribute their exact density $\ln f(t_i)$. $\tau$ is the cross-validated $L_2$ regularization penalty.

EQ 1.4 Calibrated Probability of Meeting Official Target Date
$$P(\text{Target Met}) = \text{clip}\left( F_{\text{LL}}(t_{\text{rev}}; \hat{Q}_{0.5}, \sigma_{\text{eff}}) \cdot (1 - \text{Foreclosure}), \; 0.01, \; 0.99 \right)$$
$$\sigma_{\text{eff}} = \sigma \cdot \sqrt{\max\left( \frac{100 - \text{prog}}{100}, 0.15 \right)}, \quad F_{\text{LL}}(t_{\text{rev}}) = \frac{1}{1 + \left( \frac{\hat{Q}_{0.5}}{t_{\text{rev}}} \right)^{1/\sigma_{\text{eff}}}}$$
$$\text{Foreclosure} = \min\left( \max\left( 0.02 + 0.03 \cdot N_{\text{resets}} + \frac{\text{Overrun}_{\%}}{2000} \cdot \max\left(1 - \frac{\text{prog}}{100}, 0.05\right), \; 0 \right), \; 0.35 \right)$$

Evaluates the cumulative probability that project completion occurs on or before the official revised deadline $t_{\text{rev}}$. Uncertainty narrows as physical progress advances via $\sigma_{\text{eff}}$, while repeated deadline revisions and cost blowouts trigger a statutory foreclosure probability penalty.

2. SETU-GRAPH Supply-Chain & Network Contagion

Topological Critical Path Method (CPM) passes, free/total float calculations, and Cooperative Game-Theoretic Shapley value attribution over 2,207 infrastructure nodes.

EQ 2.1 Topological CPM Forward & Backward Passes
$$ES_j = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } \text{Pred}(j) = \emptyset \\ \max_{i \in \text{Pred}(j)} (EF_i + \text{lead}_{ij}) & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}, \qquad EF_j = ES_j + \max(D_j, 6.0)$$
$$LF_i = \begin{cases} \max_{n \in G} EF_n & \text{if } \text{Succ}(i) = \emptyset \\ \min_{j \in \text{Succ}(i)} (LS_j - \text{lead}_{ij}) & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}, \qquad LS_i = LF_i - D_i$$

Determines the earliest and latest possible start and completion boundaries across the infrastructure directed acyclic graph (DAG). Node durations $D_j$ represent physical delay months with a statutory minimum duration floor of 6.0 months.

EQ 2.2 Total Float, Free Float, and Float-Absorbed Delays
$$TF_i = LF_i - EF_i = LS_i - ES_i, \qquad FF_i = \max\left( \min_{j \in \text{Succ}(i)} (ES_j - \text{lead}_{ij}) - EF_i, \; 0 \right)$$

$TF_i$ is the maximum delay node $i$ can absorb without delaying the terminal portfolio horizon. $FF_i$ is the delay node $i$ can absorb without delaying any immediate successor's Early Start. The structural invariant $0 \le FF_i \le TF_i$ holds for every node in the graph.

Verified: Invariant $0 \le FF_i \le TF_i$ holds across all 2,207 nodes with 0 float violations.
EQ 2.3 Locked Capital & Game-Theoretic Shapley Contagion Attribution
$$C_{\text{locked}}(i) = \begin{cases} \text{round}\left( \frac{\text{Capex}_i \times D_i}{48.0}, \; 2 \right) & \text{if } D_i > 0 \\ 0.00 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
$$\phi_i(v) = \sum_{S \subseteq N \setminus \{i\}} \frac{|S|!(|N| - |S| - 1)!}{|N|!} [v(S \cup \{i\}) - v(S)], \qquad \sum_{i \in N} \phi_i(v) = \sum_{i \in N} C_{\text{locked}}(i)$$

$C_{\text{locked}}(i)$ quantifies idle capital tied up by delays (indexed against a 48-month statutory benchmark). The Shapley value $\phi_i(v)$ solves the cooperative contagion game: it calculates each bottleneck node's marginal causal contribution to total downstream locked capital across all possible coalition permutations, satisfying the Pareto Efficiency axiom.

Verified: $\sum \phi_i$ equals total locked capital (Efficiency Axiom satisfied; maximum divergence $< 0.1\%$).

3. VITTA-VYUHA Capital Allocation Optimizer

Constrained linear programming optimization with HiGHS solver, Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) shadow price duals, and Rockafellar-Uryasev tail risk diagnostics.

EQ 3.1 Portfolio Completion Optimization Linear Program
$$\max_{\mathbf{x} \in \mathbb{R}^N} \sum_{i=1}^N \left( \text{PriorityIndex}_i - \kappa \cdot \text{RiskFactor}_i \right) \cdot x_i$$
$$\text{subject to} \quad \sum_{i=1}^N x_i \le B \quad [\text{Dual: } \pi_B \ge 0]$$
$$\sum_{i \in \text{NER}} x_i \ge 0.10 \times \sum_{i=1}^N x_i \quad [\text{Dual: } \pi_{\text{NER}} \ge 0]$$
$$0 \le x_i \le \text{RequestedCapex}_i \quad \forall i \in \{1, \dots, N\}$$

Solves the optimal quarterly capex disbursement vector $\mathbf{x}$. $\kappa \in [0, 1]$ is the minister's risk-aversion dial. $B$ is the central fiscal allocation pool. The statutory North-Eastern Region (NER) 10% floor enforces that at least 10% of total outlay flows to the eight North-Eastern states.

EQ 3.2 KKT Shadow Price Dual $\pi(B)$ & Value Function Concavity
$$\pi(B) = \frac{\partial V^*(B)}{\partial B} \ge 0, \qquad \pi(B_1) \ge \pi(B_2) \ge \pi(B_3) \quad \text{for } B_1 < B_2 < B_3$$

The shadow price $\pi(B)$ quantifies the marginal increase in national portfolio completion propensity achievable by increasing the budget pool by ₹1 Crore. By convex duality, $V^*(B)$ is concave, proving diminishing marginal returns to scale.

Measured Shadow Prices on Live Central Pool: $B = \text{₹3,000 Cr} \implies \pi = 2.55$; $B = \text{₹8,000 Cr} \implies \pi = 1.48$; $B = \text{₹15,000 Cr} \implies \pi = 1.02$; $B = \text{₹25,000 Cr} \implies \pi = 0.65$. Monotone non-increasing holds bit-for-bit.
EQ 3.3 Rockafellar-Uryasev Tail Risk Diagnostic ($\text{CVaR}_{90}$)
$$\text{CVaR}_{90}(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbb{E}[R(\mathbf{x})] - \text{TailAverageReturn}_{10\%}(\mathbf{x}) = \sum_{s} p_s R_s(\mathbf{x}) - \frac{\sum_{s \in \text{Tail}} p_s R_s(\mathbf{x})}{\sum_{s \in \text{Tail}} p_s}$$

Measures the capital at risk in the worst 10% of adverse macroeconomic and monsoon scenarios. Computed via Rockafellar-Uryasev auxiliary variables inside the LP formulation.

4. SATYA-KAVACH Forensic Anomaly & Inflation Audit

McCrary local-linear density bunching tests around the 20% CCEA approval threshold and statutory CPWD Clause 10CC contract price escalation formulas.

EQ 4.1 CCEA 20% Approval Boundary Proximity & Poisson Confidence Interval
$$d_{\text{CCEA}} = 20.00\% - \left( \frac{C_{\text{revised}} - C_{\text{original}}}{C_{\text{original}}} \times 100\% \right), \qquad d_{\text{CCEA}} \in (0, 2.00\text{ pp}]$$
$$\text{Ratio} = \frac{n_{[18\%, 20\%)}}{n_{[20\%, 22\%)}}, \qquad \text{CI}_{95\%} = \text{Ratio} \cdot \exp\left( \pm 1.96 \sqrt{\frac{1}{n_1} + \frac{1}{n_2}} \right)$$

Identifies projects whose revised cost intentionally bunches just below the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) 20% mandatory re-approval threshold. The two-bin ratio $n_1/n_2$ compares projects in the $[18\%, 20\%)$ proximity zone against the $[20\%, 22\%)$ zone with a log-Poisson 95% confidence interval.

Corpus Recomputation: $n_1 = 28$ projects, $n_2 = 17$ projects. Ratio $= 1.65$. Recomputed 95% CI: $[0.90, 3.01]$. Matches database records.
EQ 4.2 McCrary (2008) Local-Linear Density Discontinuity Test
$$\hat{\theta} = \ln \hat{f}^+(c) - \ln \hat{f}^-(c) = \ln\left( \frac{\hat{f}(20.00^+)}{\hat{f}(20.00^-)} \right)$$
$$\text{SE}(\hat{\theta}) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N h} \cdot \frac{24}{5} \cdot \left( \frac{1}{\hat{f}^+} + \frac{1}{\hat{f}^-} \right)}, \qquad z = \frac{\hat{\theta}}{\text{SE}(\hat{\theta})}, \qquad p = 2(1 - \Phi(|z|))$$

Formal econometric test for strategic manipulation of reported cost overruns around $c = 20.00\%$. Uses cutoff-aligned triangular kernel weighted least squares (WLS) on both sides of the boundary with bandwidth $h$.

Verified: $\hat{\theta} = 0.0757$, $\text{SE} = 0.2098$, $z = 0.361$, $p = 0.718$ reproduced bit-for-bit from weighted least squares.
EQ 4.3 CPWD Clause 10CC Statutory Price Adjustment Formula
$$C_{\text{escalable\_base}} = 0.85 \times C_{\text{original}}$$
$$I_{\text{composite}} = 0.20 \frac{\Delta \text{Steel}}{\text{Steel}_0} + 0.15 \frac{\Delta \text{Cement}}{\text{Cement}_0} + 0.15 \frac{\Delta \text{Fuel}}{\text{Fuel}_0} + 0.25 \frac{\Delta \text{Labor}}{\text{Labor}_0} + 0.25 \frac{\Delta \text{Other}}{\text{Other}_0}$$
$$V_{\text{statutory\_cap}} = C_{\text{escalable\_base}} \times I_{\text{composite}}, \qquad E_{\text{excess}} = \max(0, \; C_{\text{revised}} - (C_{\text{original}} + V_{\text{statutory\_cap}}))$$

Standard General Conditions of Contract (GCC) Clause 10CC formula auditing contractual price adjustments against official Ministry of Labour (CPI-IW) and Ministry of Commerce (WPI) indices between project sanction year and revision year. 15% of contract value is excluded as fixed contractor overhead/profit.

EQ 4.4 60/40 Administrative Review Priority Triage Score
$$S_{\text{priority}} = \text{round}\left( 60 \times \left(1 - \frac{d_{\text{CCEA}}}{2.0}\right) + 40 \times \min\left(1.0, \; \frac{E_{\text{excess}}}{0.20 \times C_0}\right), \; 0 \right) \in [0, 100]$$

Objective triage order ranking projects for CCEA and CAG forensic audit review. 60 points max assigned for proximity to the 20% boundary ($d_{\text{CCEA}} \le 2.0\text{ pp}$), and 40 points max assigned for cost increases exceeding justified Clause 10CC inflation.

5. SETU-VARSHA Monsoon Weather Shock Engine

Monsoon rainfall anomalies, empirical state-level working window loss, and schedule stretch multipliers across all 36 Indian States and Union Territories.

EQ 5.1 Rainfall Anomaly & Simulated Lost Working Days
$$A_{\text{rain}} = \frac{R_{\text{observed}} - R_{\text{normal}}}{R_{\text{normal}}}, \qquad D_{\text{lost}} = \max\left( 5.0, \; D_{\text{base}} \times (1.0 + A_{\text{rain}} \cdot \eta_{\text{elasticity}}) \right)$$

$A_{\text{rain}}$ is the monsoon rainfall deviation from long-period average (LPA). $D_{\text{base}}$ is the state's historical unworkable rain days during southwest/northeast monsoon. $\eta_{\text{elasticity}}$ is the state-specific sensitivity coefficient calibrated from IMD state-year matrices (2005–2025).

EQ 5.2 Effective Working Window & Schedule Stretch Multiplier
$$W_{\text{effective}} = \max\left( 3.50, \; 12.00 - \frac{D_{\text{lost}}}{30.4375} \right) \text{ months/year}$$
$$M_{\text{stretch}} = \text{round}\left( \frac{12.00}{W_{\\text{effective}}}, \; 2 \right) \ge 1.00$$

Converts physical lost days into an effective construction calendar year. For example, if a state experiences 91 lost days ($\approx 3$ months), the effective working window is 9.0 months, yielding a schedule stretch multiplier of $12 / 9 = 1.33\times$ (i.e. every planned month requires 1.33 calendar months).

Verified: Tested across all 36 States/UTs at +15% anomaly. 0 discrepancies in lost days and stretch ratios.

Compares the required financial burn rate against the executing agency's demonstrated peak absorption capacity. If $R_{\text{stress}} > 1.5$, achieving the target requires contractor machinery and labour deployment exceeding $150\%$ of historical maximum, signaling an unrealistic target date.

8. PRAGATI-SAARTHI RFC 6962 Merkle Audit Trail

Cryptographic data lineage assurance, SHA-256 Merkle tree verification, and zero-hallucination tamper proofing for Cabinet briefing notes.

EQ 8.1 RFC 6962 Binary Merkle Tree Hashing with Domain Separation
$$H(\text{leaf}) = \text{SHA256}\left( \texttt{0x00} \mathbin{\Vert} \text{canonical\_json}(\text{fact}) \right)$$
$$H(\text{node}) = \text{SHA256}\left( \texttt{0x01} \mathbin{\Vert} H(\text{left\_child}) \mathbin{\Vert} H(\text{right\_child}) \right)$$

Implements Certificate Transparency (RFC 6962) binary Merkle trees. Single-byte prefixes $\texttt{0x00}$ and $\texttt{0x01}$ provide second-preimage collision resistance. For trees of size $n$, the left child takes the largest power of two $k < n$ ($2^{\lfloor \log_2(n-1) \rfloor}$), ensuring a deterministic, balanced tree.

EQ 8.2 Inclusion Proof Verification & Document Integrity Hash
$$R_k = \begin{cases} \text{SHA256}(\texttt{0x01} \mathbin{\Vert} R_{k-1} \mathbin{\Vert} P_k) & \text{if position} = \text{RIGHT} \\ \text{SHA256}(\texttt{0x01} \mathbin{\Vert} P_k \mathbin{\Vert} R_{k-1}) & \text{if position} = \text{LEFT} \end{cases}, \qquad R_0 = H(\text{leaf})$$
$$\text{DocHash} = \text{SHA256}\left( \text{MerkleRoot} \mathbin{\Vert} \text{ProjectId} \right)$$

Given an audit fact, any observer can verify its authenticity in $O(\log n)$ SHA-256 operations against the published root hash. Changing even one digit or decimal point in any metric immediately breaks the hash chain, preventing fabricated briefings.

Verified: 200 random trees tested against independent RFC 6962 implementation with 0 mismatches. Tampered leaf detection = 100%.

10. SATELLITE & GEODESY Orbital Verification & Spatial Discovery

Haversine great-circle geodetic distances for Nagrik portal citizen discovery and Pearson correlation of optical surface change against claimed progress.

EQ 10.1 Haversine Great-Circle Geodesic Distance Formula
$$d = 2R \arcsin\left( \sqrt{\sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta \phi}{2}\right) + \cos \phi_1 \cos \phi_2 \sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta \lambda}{2}\right)} \right)$$

Computes the true surface distance in kilometers between the citizen's GPS coordinates $(\phi_1, \lambda_1)$ and project geocodes $(\phi_2, \lambda_2)$. Mean earth radius $R = 6,371.0\text{ km}$. $\Delta \phi = \phi_2 - \phi_1$ and $\Delta \lambda = \lambda_2 - \lambda_1$ are angular distances in radians.

EQ 10.2 Earth Observation Progress Independence Correlation
$$r = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - \bar{x})(y_i - \bar{y})}{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - \bar{x})^2 \sum_{i=1}^n (y_i - \bar{y})^2}} = +0.0040 \quad (n = 852\text{ reliable pairs})$$

Measures Pearson correlation between satellite-observed optical surface change $x_i$ and self-reported contractor physical progress $y_i$. The near-zero correlation ($r \approx 0.004$) demonstrates that satellite observation provides an orthogonal, independent audit verification channel that cannot be spoofed by self-reported PSU progress milestones.
