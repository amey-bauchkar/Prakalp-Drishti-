1. KAAL-CHAKRA Temporal Risk Engine

Accelerated Failure Time (AFT) log-logistic survival regression combined with split-conformal quantile forecasting for project completion timelines.

EQ 1.1 Log-Logistic Survival & Cumulative Hazard Functions
$$S(t) = \frac{1}{1 + \left(\frac{t}{b}\right)^a} = \frac{1}{1 + e^z}, \qquad H(t) = -\ln S(t) = \ln(1 + e^z)$$

Models the probability that a project duration exceeds $t$ months from sanction. $z = \frac{\ln(t) - \mu}{\sigma}$ is the standardized log-time deviation, where $\sigma = 1/a$ is the scale parameter and $\mu = \ln(b)$ is the median duration predictor.

EQ 1.2 AFT Penalized Log-Likelihood with Right-Censoring
$$\mathcal{L}_{\text{pen}}(\boldsymbol{\theta}) = -\sum_{i=1}^N \left[ e_i \ln f(t_i \mid \mathbf{x}_i) + (1 - e_i) \ln S(t_i \mid \mathbf{x}_i) \right] + \frac{1}{2\tau^2} \sum_{g \in \text{sector} \cup \text{entity}} \beta_g^2$$

Objective minimized via L-BFGS-B over $N = 2,103$ projects ($ 115$ observed completions, $1,988$ right-censored; $45$ rows whose completion proxy lies after the as-of date are excluded as not yet observed). Ongoing projects ($e_i = 0$) contribute $\ln S(t_i)$; completed projects ($e_i = 1$) contribute $\ln f(t_i)$. The penalty is a Normal$(0, \tau^2)$ prior on the sector and entity effects only, with $\tau$ chosen by 5-fold cross-validated held-out log-likelihood. The completion proxy is the final revised target date: MoSPI records no actual completion date, and the register sheds completed projects, so the baseline multiplier is an upper-ish bound (see survivorship in the artefact).

EQ 1.3 Conformalized Fan Quantile Prediction Bounds
$$\hat{Q}_\alpha(x) = \max \left( \text{cummax}\left( \hat{T}_{\text{raw}} \cdot \exp(q_\alpha) \right), \; t_{\text{curr}} + c_\alpha \cdot \max\left( \frac{100 - \text{prog}}{100}, 0.15 \right) \right)$$

Split-conformal calibration of the engine's own raw median: $q_\alpha$ is the finite-sample quantile (rank $\lceil (n+1)\alpha \rceil$) of the residual $r = \ln(\text{official target} / \hat{T}_{\text{raw}})$ over $596$ forward-looking calibration projects. All four multipliers are below 1: the raw AFT / earned-value median runs late against official targets, and the calibration pulls it in. The fan is rearranged to be monotone and floored so no quantile pre-dates time already elapsed. Overdue projects ($616$) are excluded from calibration because an overdue official target is censored, not observed, information.

EQ 1.4 Calibrated Probability of Meeting Official Target Date
$$P(\text{Target Met}) = \text{clip}\left( F_{\text{LL}}(t_{\text{rev}}; \hat{Q}_{0.5}, \sigma_{\text{eff}}) \cdot (1 - \text{Foreclosure}), \; 0.01, \; 0.99 \right)$$
$$\sigma_{\text{eff}} = \sigma \cdot \sqrt{\max\left( \frac{100 - \text{prog}}{100}, 0.15 \right)}, \quad F_{\text{LL}}(t_{\text{rev}}) = \frac{1}{1 + \left( \frac{\hat{Q}_{0.5}}{t_{\text{rev}}} \right)^{1/\sigma_{\text{eff}}}}$$
$$\text{Foreclosure} = \min\left( \max\left( 0.02 + 0.03 \cdot N_{\text{resets}} + \frac{\text{Overrun}_{\%}}{2000} \cdot \max\left(1 - \frac{\text{prog}}{100}, 0.05\right), \; 0 \right), \; 0.35 \right)$$

Evaluates the cumulative probability that project completion occurs on or before the official revised deadline $t_{\text{rev}}$. Uncertainty narrows as physical progress advances via $\sigma_{\text{eff}}$, while repeated deadline revisions and cost blowouts trigger a statutory foreclosure probability penalty.

2. SETU-GRAPH Supply-Chain & Network Contagion

Topological Critical Path Method (CPM) passes, free/total float calculations, and Cooperative Game-Theoretic Shapley value attribution over 2,207 infrastructure nodes.

EQ 2.1 Topological CPM Forward & Backward Passes
$$ES_j = \begin{cases} 0 & \text{if } \text{Pred}(j) = \emptyset \\ \max_{i \in \text{Pred}(j)} (EF_i + \text{lead}_{ij}) & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}, \qquad EF_j = ES_j + \max(D_j, 6.0)$$
$$LF_i = \begin{cases} \max_{n \in \text{comp}(i)} EF_n & \text{if } \text{Succ}(i) = \emptyset \\ \min_{j \in \text{Succ}(i)} (LS_j - \text{lead}_{ij}) & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}, \qquad LS_i = LF_i - \max(D_i, 6.0)$$

Determines the earliest and latest possible start and completion boundaries across the infrastructure directed acyclic graph (DAG). Node durations $D_j$ are observed delay months with an engine floor of 6.0 months (a modelling floor, not a statutory one). The late finish of a sink is the finish of its own weakly-connected component, so an isolated project is never measured against an unrelated corridor's horizon. Verified: ES/EF reproduced on every node.

EQ 2.2 Total Float, Free Float, and Float-Absorbed Delays
$$TF_i = LF_i - EF_i = LS_i - ES_i, \qquad FF_i = \max\left( \min_{j \in \text{Succ}(i)} (ES_j - \text{lead}_{ij}) - EF_i, \; 0 \right)$$

$TF_i$ is the maximum delay node $i$ can absorb without delaying the terminal portfolio horizon. $FF_i$ is the delay node $i$ can absorb without delaying any immediate successor's Early Start. The structural invariant $0 \le FF_i \le TF_i$ holds for every node in the graph.

Verified: Invariant $0 \le FF_i \le TF_i$ holds across all 2,207 nodes with 0 float violations.
EQ 2.3 Locked Capital & Game-Theoretic Shapley Contagion Attribution
$$C_{\text{locked}}(i) = \begin{cases} \text{round}\left( \frac{\text{Capex}_i \times D_i}{48.0}, \; 2 \right) & \text{if } D_i > 0 \\ 0.00 & \text{otherwise} \end{cases}$$
$$\phi_i(v) = \sum_{S \subseteq N \setminus \{i\}} \frac{|S|!(|N| - |S| - 1)!}{|N|!} [v(S \cup \{i\}) - v(S)], \qquad \sum_{i \in N} \phi_i(v) = \sum_{i \in N} C_{\text{locked}}(i)$$

$C_{\text{locked}}(i)$ is a policy normalisation, not a model output: sanctioned capital counts as fully locked at 48 months of delay and linearly below. The Shapley value is taken over the reachability game $v(S) = \sum \text{cost}$ over $\bigcup_{i \in S}(\{i\} \cup \text{desc}(i))$, estimated by permutation Monte Carlo ($M = 300$, seeded). Because $\text{reach}(i)$ includes $i$, a sink is worth $\text{cost}/(1 + \#\text{ancestors})$ and an isolated project exactly its own cost. The final rescaling to locked-capital units is a unit conversion; efficiency itself holds because per-permutation marginals telescope to $v(N)$.

Verified: on a 6-node DAG the estimator matches exact enumeration within 0.006 of share and reproduces the sink closed form; the persisted shapley.parquet equals a fresh seeded compute (max diff 0.0); $\sum \phi_i$ equals total locked capital within 0.1%. Edges are inferred (275 sector supply-chain + 1,070 geo-adjacency $\le$ 50 km), each carrying its basis; 1,050 of 2,207 projects are isolated.

3. VITTA-VYUHA Capital Allocation Optimizer

Constrained linear programming optimization with HiGHS solver, Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) shadow price duals, and Rockafellar-Uryasev tail risk diagnostics.

EQ 3.1 Portfolio Completion Optimization Linear Program
$$\min_{\mathbf{x}, \eta, \boldsymbol{\zeta}} \; -(1-\kappa) \sum_{i=1}^N \sum_{t=1}^3 \bar{r}_{it} \, x_{it} \;+\; \kappa \left[ \eta + \frac{1}{1-\alpha} \sum_{s=1}^3 p_s \zeta_s \right], \qquad \zeta_s \ge -\sum_{i,t} r_{sit} x_{it} - \eta, \; \zeta_s \ge 0, \; \eta \text{ free}$$
$$\text{subject to} \quad \sum_{i=1}^N x_i \le B \quad [\text{Dual: } \pi_B \ge 0]$$
$$\sum_{i \in \text{NER}} x_i \ge 0.10 \times \sum_{i=1}^N x_i \quad [\text{Dual: } \pi_{\text{NER}} \ge 0]$$
$$0 \le x_{it} \le u_{it} = \text{demand}_i \cdot f_t, \quad f = (0.40, 0.35, 0.25), \qquad \frac{x_{i,t+1}}{u_{i,t+1}} \le \frac{x_{it}}{u_{it}} \quad (\text{tranche fill order})$$

A convex mean–CVaR trade-off, both terms in the same yield-weighted-Cr units: $\kappa = 0$ maximises expected scenario yield, $\kappa = 1$ maximises the 90% tail-average yield. $r_{sit} = \theta_{si} \cdot \text{yield}_i \cdot m_t$ with $\theta_{si} = \text{clip}(1 - \text{severity}_s \cdot \text{risk}_i, 0.05, 1.10)$, $\bar{r}_{it} = \sum_s p_s r_{sit}$, $\alpha = 0.90$. Scenarios $p = (0.50, 0.35, 0.15)$, severities $(-0.05, 0.15, 0.45)$ are declared policy, not estimated (no realised-yield history exists), and are returned on every result under cvar_diagnostics. Every variable is continuous (HiGHS via milp with integrality = 0), so this is an LP with valid duals; $B$ is the pool; the NER floor is the statutory 10%. Verified: budget, tranche bounds and NER floor hold at every (B, κ) tested; complementary slackness $\max|\text{slack} \cdot \text{dual}| = 5.7 \times 10^{-14}$.

EQ 3.2 KKT Shadow Price Dual $\pi(B)$ & Value Function Concavity
$$\pi(B) = \frac{\partial V^*(B)}{\partial B} \ge 0, \qquad \pi(B_1) \ge \pi(B_2) \ge \pi(B_3) \quad \text{for } B_1 < B_2 < B_3$$

The shadow price $\pi(B)$ is the LP dual of the budget row: the marginal objective value (yield-weighted Cr) of one more ₹1 Cr in the pool. The LP value function is concave in $B$, so $\pi$ is non-increasing. The figures below are the duals HiGHS returned at $\kappa = 0.5$; none is synthesised, and if the solve fails no dual is reported.

Measured Shadow Prices on Live Central Pool: $B = \text{₹3,000 Cr} \implies \pi = 2.55$; $B = \text{₹8,000 Cr} \implies \pi = 1.48$; $B = \text{₹15,000 Cr} \implies \pi = 1.02$; $B = \text{₹25,000 Cr} \implies \pi = 0.65$. Monotone non-increasing holds on these four solves.
EQ 3.3 Rockafellar-Uryasev Tail Risk Diagnostic ($\text{CVaR}_{90}$)
$$\text{CVaR}_{90}(\mathbf{x}) = \mathbb{E}[R(\mathbf{x})] - \text{TailAverageReturn}_{10\%}(\mathbf{x}) = \sum_{s} p_s R_s(\mathbf{x}) - \frac{\sum_{s \in \text{Tail}} p_s R_s(\mathbf{x})}{\sum_{s \in \text{Tail}} p_s}$$

Reported tail loss = expected scenario return minus the 90% tail-average return of the solved allocation, on a κ-invariant yardstick (κ = 0 tranche multipliers) so two calls with different κ are comparable. With $p_{\text{severe}} = 0.15 > 1 - \alpha = 0.10$ the tail lies inside the severe-slippage scenario, so $\text{CVaR}_{0.90}$ equals that scenario's outcome and $\zeta = 0$ at the optimum — the correct Rockafellar–Uryasev solution for a three-point distribution. The three scenarios are delivery-slippage states, not macroeconomic or monsoon scenarios. Verified: the auxiliary variables reproduce the tail computed directly from scenario returns on every call; tail loss falls monotonically as κ rises.

4. SATYA-KAVACH Forensic Anomaly & Inflation Audit

McCrary local-linear density bunching tests around the 20% CCEA approval threshold and statutory CPWD Clause 10CC contract price escalation formulas.

EQ 4.1 CCEA 20% Approval Boundary Proximity & Poisson Confidence Interval
$$d_{\text{CCEA}} = 20.00\% - \left( \frac{C_{\text{revised}} - C_{\text{original}}}{C_{\text{original}}} \times 100\% \right), \qquad d_{\text{CCEA}} \in (0, 2.00\text{ pp}]$$
$$\text{Ratio} = \frac{n_{[18\%, 20\%)}}{n_{[20\%, 22\%)}}, \qquad \text{CI}_{95\%} = \text{Ratio} \cdot \exp\left( \pm 1.96 \sqrt{\frac{1}{n_1} + \frac{1}{n_2}} \right)$$

Counts revisions that land just below the Cabinet Committee on Economic Affairs (CCEA) 20% re-approval threshold. It is a descriptive count and says nothing about intent. The two-bin ratio $n_1/n_2$ compares projects in the $[18\%, 20\%)$ proximity zone against the $[20\%, 22\%)$ zone with a log-Poisson 95% confidence interval.

Corpus Recomputation: $n_1 = 28$ projects, $n_2 = 17$ projects. Ratio $= 1.65$. Recomputed 95% CI: $[0.90, 3.01]$. Matches the engine. The interval spans 1.0 and the ratio moves with the bin width (1.22× at 1 pt, 1.65× at 2 pt, 1.49× at 5 pt): descriptive only.
EQ 4.2 McCrary (2008) Local-Linear Density Discontinuity Test
$$\hat{\theta} = \ln \hat{f}^+(c) - \ln \hat{f}^-(c) = \ln\left( \frac{\hat{f}(20.00^+)}{\hat{f}(20.00^-)} \right)$$
$$\text{SE}(\hat{\theta}) = \sqrt{\frac{1}{N h} \cdot \frac{24}{5} \cdot \left( \frac{1}{\hat{f}^+} + \frac{1}{\hat{f}^-} \right)}, \qquad z = \frac{\hat{\theta}}{\text{SE}(\hat{\theta})}, \qquad p = 2(1 - \Phi(|z|))$$

McCrary (2008) density-discontinuity test at $c = 20\%$: cutoff-aligned histogram (bin width $b = 2\hat{\sigma} N^{-1/2}$), triangular-kernel local-linear density on each side, plug-in bandwidth capped at one standard deviation (the uncapped plug-in rejects a smooth null 96% of the time; capped, empirical size is 7% at nominal 5% and power 99% against +5% planted mass — simulated in tests/test_math_audit.py). A significant $\theta < 0$ would show the threshold shapes where revisions land; it would not show intent.

Live corpus, $N = 1,138$, $h = 34.8$: $\hat{\theta} = +0.0757$, $\text{SE} = 0.1838$, $z = 0.412$, $p = 0.6806$ two-sided, $p = 0.6597$ one-sided for bunching below — no density discontinuity at 20%. Verified: $\hat{\theta}$, SE, $z$ and $p$ recomputed with an independent weighted least-squares fit.
EQ 4.3 CPWD Clause 10CC Statutory Price Adjustment Formula
$$C_{\text{escalable\_base}} = 0.85 \times C_{\text{original}}$$
$$I_{\text{composite}} = 0.20 \frac{\Delta \text{Steel}}{\text{Steel}_0} + 0.15 \frac{\Delta \text{Cement}}{\text{Cement}_0} + 0.15 \frac{\Delta \text{Fuel}}{\text{Fuel}_0} + 0.25 \frac{\Delta \text{Labor}}{\text{Labor}_0} + 0.25 \frac{\Delta \text{Other}}{\text{Other}_0}$$
$$V_{\text{statutory\_cap}} = C_{\text{escalable\_base}} \times I_{\text{composite}}, \qquad E_{\text{excess}} = \max(0, \; C_{\text{revised}} - (C_{\text{original}} + V_{\text{statutory\_cap}}))$$

Standard General Conditions of Contract (GCC) Clause 10CC formula auditing contractual price adjustments against official Ministry of Labour (CPI-IW) and Ministry of Commerce (WPI) indices between project sanction year and revision year. 15% of contract value is excluded as fixed contractor overhead/profit.

EQ 4.4 60/40 Administrative Review Priority Triage Score
$$S_{\text{priority}} = \text{round}\left( 60 \times \left(1 - \frac{d_{\text{CCEA}}}{2.0}\right) + 40 \times \min\left(1.0, \; \frac{E_{\text{excess}}}{0.15 \times C_0}\right), \; 0 \right) \in [0, 100]$$

A review-priority order for the [18%, 20%) band: up to 60 points for proximity to the threshold and up to 40 for revised cost above the Clause 10CC cap, saturating at 15% of original cost. The 60/40 split is a declared policy weight; nothing is fitted. It orders which files are opened first — it is not a probability and not a finding, and intent is never inferred.

5. SETU-VARSHA Monsoon Weather Shock Engine

Monsoon rainfall anomalies, empirical state-level working window loss, and schedule stretch multipliers across all 36 Indian States and Union Territories.

EQ 5.1 Rainfall Anomaly & Simulated Lost Working Days
$$A_{\text{rain}} = \frac{R_{\text{observed}} - R_{\text{normal}}}{R_{\text{normal}}}, \qquad D_{\text{lost}} = \max\left( 5.0, \; D_{\text{base}} \times (1.0 + A_{\text{rain}} \cdot \eta_{\text{elasticity}}) \right)$$

$A_{\text{rain}}$ is the monsoon rainfall deviation from long-period average (LPA). $D_{\text{base}}$ is the state's historical unworkable rain days during southwest/northeast monsoon. $\eta_{\text{elasticity}}$ and $D_{\text{base}}$ are declared expert parameters per State/UT (climatic zone and terrain), not coefficients fitted to a downtime series — no per-state construction-downtime record exists to fit against. The IMD 2005–2025 departure matrix (756 state-years) supplies the anomaly history they are applied to.

EQ 5.2 Effective Working Window & Schedule Stretch Multiplier
$$W_{\text{effective}} = \max\left( 3.50, \; 12.00 - \frac{D_{\text{lost}}}{30.4375} \right) \text{ months/year}$$
$$M_{\text{stretch}} = \text{round}\left( \frac{12.00}{W_{\\text{effective}}}, \; 2 \right) \ge 1.00$$

Converts physical lost days into an effective construction calendar year. For example, if a state experiences 91 lost days ($\approx 3$ months), the effective working window is 9.0 months, yielding a schedule stretch multiplier of $12 / 9 = 1.33\times$ (i.e. every planned month requires 1.33 calendar months).

Verified: Tested across all 36 States/UTs at +15% anomaly. 0 discrepancies in lost days and stretch ratios.

6. KARYA-DAKSHATA Execution Velocity Simulator

Re-pricing of a proposal at the executing agency's measured historical delivery multiple, with a declared-weight reliability score.

EQ 6.1 Agency Delivery Multiple & Reliability Score
$$C_{\text{expected}} = C_{\text{base}} \left( 1 + \frac{\overline{\text{overrun}}_a}{100} \right), \qquad T_{\text{expected}} = T_{\text{base}} + 30.4375 \cdot \overline{\text{delay}}_a \;[\text{days}]$$
$$\text{Reliability}_a = \max\left( 0, \; 100 - \left[ 0.6 \cdot \text{DelayRate}_a + 0.4 \cdot \frac{\min(\overline{\text{overrun}}_a, 200)}{2} \right] \right) \in [0, 100]$$
$$\overline{\text{overrun}}_a, \; \overline{\text{delay}}_a, \; \text{DelayRate}_a \; = \; \text{agency } a\text{'s mean cost overrun (\%), mean slippage (months) and share of projects delayed, over its projects in the corpus}$$

Arithmetic on the historical record, not a forecast of the specific proposal: the base cost and timeline are scaled by the agency's measured mean overrun and slippage across its projects in the corpus. The 60/40 split inside the reliability score is a declared presentation weight, not a fitted parameter, and is labelled so in the payload (basis).

8. PRAGATI-SAARTHI RFC 6962 Merkle Audit Trail

Cryptographic data lineage assurance, SHA-256 Merkle tree verification, and zero-hallucination tamper proofing for Cabinet briefing notes.

EQ 8.1 RFC 6962 Binary Merkle Tree Hashing with Domain Separation
$$H(\text{leaf}) = \text{SHA256}\left( \texttt{0x00} \mathbin{\Vert} \text{canonical\_json}(\text{fact}) \right)$$
$$H(\text{node}) = \text{SHA256}\left( \texttt{0x01} \mathbin{\Vert} H(\text{left\_child}) \mathbin{\Vert} H(\text{right\_child}) \right)$$

Implements Certificate Transparency (RFC 6962) binary Merkle trees. Single-byte prefixes $\texttt{0x00}$ and $\texttt{0x01}$ provide second-preimage collision resistance. For trees of size $n$, the left child takes the largest power of two $k < n$ ($2^{\lfloor \log_2(n-1) \rfloor}$), ensuring a deterministic, balanced tree.

EQ 8.2 Inclusion Proof Verification & Document Integrity Hash
$$R_k = \begin{cases} \text{SHA256}(\texttt{0x01} \mathbin{\Vert} R_{k-1} \mathbin{\Vert} P_k) & \text{if position} = \text{RIGHT} \\ \text{SHA256}(\texttt{0x01} \mathbin{\Vert} P_k \mathbin{\Vert} R_{k-1}) & \text{if position} = \text{LEFT} \end{cases}, \qquad R_0 = H(\text{leaf})$$
$$\text{DocHash} = \text{SHA256}\left( \text{MerkleRoot} \mathbin{\Vert} \text{ProjectId} \right)$$

Given an audit fact, any observer can verify its authenticity in $O(\log n)$ SHA-256 operations against the published root hash. Changing even one digit or decimal point in any metric immediately breaks the hash chain, preventing fabricated briefings.

Verified: 200 random trees tested against independent RFC 6962 implementation with 0 mismatches. Tampered leaf detection = 100%.

10. SATELLITE & GEODESY Orbital Verification & Spatial Discovery

Haversine great-circle geodetic distances for Nagrik portal citizen discovery and Pearson correlation of optical surface change against claimed progress.

EQ 10.1 Haversine Great-Circle Geodesic Distance Formula
$$d = 2R \arcsin\left( \sqrt{\sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta \phi}{2}\right) + \cos \phi_1 \cos \phi_2 \sin^2\left(\frac{\Delta \lambda}{2}\right)} \right)$$

Computes the true surface distance in kilometers between the citizen's GPS coordinates $(\phi_1, \lambda_1)$ and project geocodes $(\phi_2, \lambda_2)$. Mean earth radius $R = 6,371.0\text{ km}$. $\Delta \phi = \phi_2 - \phi_1$ and $\Delta \lambda = \lambda_2 - \lambda_1$ are angular distances in radians.

EQ 10.2 Earth Observation Progress Independence Correlation
$$r = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - \bar{x})(y_i - \bar{y})}{\sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n (x_i - \bar{x})^2 \sum_{i=1}^n (y_i - \bar{y})^2}} = +0.0040 \quad (n = 1,588\text{ site-level projects})$$

Measures Pearson correlation between satellite-observed optical surface change $x_i$ and self-reported contractor physical progress $y_i$. The near-zero correlation ($r = +0.004$, $p = 0.87$; Spearman $\rho = +0.024$) is the reason no satellite-derived completion percentage is computed anywhere: imagery carries no information about the reported completion fraction, so the layer reports activity evidence, never a percentage. The figure is persisted with the catalogue's hash in artifacts/eo_progress_independence.json and recomputed by eo_independence.py.
